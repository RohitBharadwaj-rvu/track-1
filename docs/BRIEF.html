1. Unique Selling Proposition

Instead of off-the-shelf Stable-Baselines3 pixel CNNs—which overfit to wall textures and strain the 2 GB VRAM / 1 CPU sandbox—we built a 100% custom PyTorch + NumPy architecture and RL pipeline from scratch. Operating directly on doom_env geometric telemetry (render_buffers=False) reaches 301.7 decisions/sec on A100 while pairing a compact recurrent policy with an odometric 2D spatial memory built strictly from legal observations. Every action comes from trained weights (zero if/else action trees, zero inference pathfinding, zero aimbots, zero edits to env/).

2. System Architecture & Inference Pipeline

Runtime Lifecycle (submission/agent.py): __init__() warms both populated and empty-room branches so step 1 avoids CUDA init latency; reset() zeroes the GRU, spatial grid, and prev_action before every map.

3. Sensory & State Representation

  • rays (64, 360°): Normalized distance [d, 1−d] + 6-class one-hot hit type (none, wall, door, locked_door, enemy, item).
  • visible_entities (≤32): 12-class type embedding + ego combat kinematics (distance, bearing, closing & lateral speed, time-to-impact, crosshair alignment).
  • vitals (17) + prev_action (9): Health, armor, weapon one-hot, ammo, 6 key flags, velocity, plus explicit empty_ammo / low_health / speed cues for snag detection & edge-triggered USE.
  • rgb & depth: Intentionally omitted for texture invariance and speed (switch/nukage trade-off discussed on Page 2).

4. Neural Architecture (884k Params)

  • Circular 1D CNN (CircularRayEncoder): Three circular-padded 1D conv layers preserve 360° left-right continuity with no wraparound seam.
  • Variable-Enemy Cross-Attention: Permutation-invariant 4-head, 4-query masked attention conditioned on vitals + rays, combined with per-category enemy/item counts and a learned empty-room fallback—cleanly handling 0 to 32 entities.
  • Factorized Actor & Rocket Mask: Three 3-way categoricals (move, strafe, turn → 0 conflicting buttons) + 3 Bernoullis (ATTACK, USE, WPN) + observation-only close-range RocketLauncher safety mask.

5. Spatial Memory Design

  • Pure-NumPy Odometric 2D Grid (submission/ego_map.py): Builds a persistent 128×128 map (48u/cell) strictly from legal telemetry and prev_action (zero privileged coordinates, zero pathfinding). Integrates Doom turn-hold physics and velocity, refined by 2-iteration wall-ray ICP to prevent wall-slide drift.
  • 8 Semantic Channels & Perimeter Beacons: Tracks visited cells, a post-key visited channel (clears on each key pickup so explored halls become fresh backtracking targets), walls, free space, doors, locked doors (kept until a key is held), items/keys, and decaying enemies. Crops an ego-aligned (8, 25, 25) view into a 3-layer CNN (121k), projecting off-crop keys and locked doors onto the border ring for long-range return bearing.
  • Residual GRU (256-dim) + Auxiliary Memory Head: LN(GRU(x) + x) preserves short-term combat rhythm and USE button-edge timing; during training, AuxMemoryHead supervises nearest matching locked-door bearing (100% sighting accuracy).

6. Training & Exploration Pipeline

  • Custom Recurrent PPO (training/ppo.py): Built from scratch in PyTorch with chunked BPTT (chunk_len=64, episode-boundary hidden masking), GAE (γ=0.995), clipped Huber value loss, bf16 AMP, and 50% exact left-right mirror symmetry. Sprint 6B trained for 3.26M decisions (9,122 episodes).
  • Privileged Scaffolding (Training-Only): An AsymmetricCritic (16 privileged progress features, 0.86 explained variance) plus UDMF/binary WAD Geodesic PBRS computing shortest-path progression potentials (spawn → key/switch → lock → exit).
  • Anti-Stuck Exploration & Rewards: Exit-dominant rewards (+12 exit + time/HP bonus, +7 unlock, +4 key, +1.2 kill), diminishing cell novelty + post-key exploration reset, spin / door-bounce / USE-hold penalties, and optional RND curiosity.
  • 5,000-Map Corpus & Curriculum: Procedural UDMF/dungeon generator (multi-key joins, relays, compound gauntlets) + 9 disjoint freedoom2 training maps (0 D4 leakage with held-out suites), driven by a 3-phase auto-curriculum with SFL/PVL regret sampling, ACCEL mutation, and Do-No-Harm promotion gating.

9. Failure Analysis & Mitigations

  • Fixed — Point-Blank RocketLauncher Self-Kills: On final_ood_t3_dropoff_skull, the agent picked up a RocketLauncher near spawn and self-killed via splash damage (steps 23–38). Adding our observation-only rocket_safety_mask=True in submission/policy.py eliminated close-range self-kills without heuristic action trees.
  • Fixed — Forgetting & Over-Cautious Fine-Tuning: Post-6B sprints showed harsh death penalties suppressed key-room entry while weak KL drifted policies. We added analytical teacher KL, Model Soup selection, and a strict Do-No-Harm promotion gate (promote_checkpoint.py) that protected validated Sprint 6B.
  • Known Limitation — Wall-Switch & Nukage Aliasing: Because doom_env rays tag wall/exit switches as RAY_WALL and omit damaging floors, the telemetry-only agent pressed USE 0 times on stress/exit_switch.wad and timed out on retail Freedoom switch exits. With 74% VRAM headroom free, fusing a compact 30×40 rgb/depth CNN is our targeted next upgrade.
